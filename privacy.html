Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding how AUREX CITY collects, uses, protects, and handles Discord data.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: November 27, 2026

01 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how AUREX CITY (Application ID: 1575648484814618765, "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, stores, and protects information when you use our bot within Discord servers or interact with its commands.

We are committed to user privacy and strictly comply with the Discord Developer Policy and Developer Terms of Service.

02 Data We Collect

AUREX CITY operates on the principle of data minimization. We only collect the minimal data necessary to deliver core bot functionality:

  • User Identifiers: Discord User IDs, usernames, and avatars to process command interactions, manage server warnings, and identify ticket authors.
  • Server & Channel Identifiers: Guild IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs to store guild-specific configurations (welcome channels, logging channels, auto-roles, and ticket categories).
  • Ticket Transcripts & Support Logs: When a support ticket is created and closed, message logs and transcripts within that specific ticket channel may be generated and stored temporarily or delivered to the designated staff log channel for audit and moderation review.
  • Virtual Economy Data: User ID, virtual balance, daily reward streak timestamps, and virtual transaction logs within the server's economy simulation.
  • Moderation Logs: User IDs, moderator IDs, warning reasons, and timestamps recorded when staff utilize moderation commands (kick, ban, warn, clear).
  • Temporary Voice State: Channel ID and User ID when using dynamic or temporary voice room features to create and delete channels on demand.

03 Data We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute transparency about what we do not touch:

  • We do not read, inspect, or log private messages (Direct Messages / DMs).
  • We do not collect personal identifying information (real names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers).
  • We do not process or store financial payment details, credit card numbers, or banking credentials.
  • We do not record, store, or eavesdrop on voice chat audio.
  • We do not collect message content outside of explicit command interactions and designated ticket support channels.

04 How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Executing requested bot commands and interactive components (buttons, dropdowns, modals).
  • Maintaining guild settings, automated role assignments, and welcome messages.
  • Facilitating customer support workflows, ticket creation, claim mechanisms, and transcript generation.
  • Maintaining fair virtual economy balances and leaderboard calculations.
  • Ensuring server security, anti-abuse protection, and moderation compliance.

05 Data Sharing & Third Parties

Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data is only shared in the following limited technical contexts:

  • Discord Inc.: All bot operations interact via the official Discord API and Gateway subject to Discord's privacy standards.
  • Hosting Infrastructure: Secure servers hosting the bot process and database storage with industry-standard encryption and access controls.
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly required by law, subpoena, or lawful process to protect our rights or the safety of users.

06 Data Retention & Storage Security

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services to the server:

  • Guild configuration data is retained while the Bot remains a member of the guild.
  • Virtual economy and warning records are stored until reset by server administrators or upon deletion request.
  • Support ticket transcripts are retained according to the server's designated retention schedule or staff log configuration.
  • All data is stored in secured, access-restricted environments protected against unauthorized access.

07 User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)

In accordance with global data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA) and Discord Developer policies, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the data associated with your Discord User ID.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): You may request the permanent removal of all stored records associated with your User ID (e.g., economy data, warnings, ticket logs).
  • Server Data Purge: Server owners may request the complete deletion of all guild-related data upon removing the Bot from their server.
To submit a data access or deletion request, join our official Discord server and open a ticket, or contact the team administrator (Ghost / ID: 1365507339368792077). Deletion requests are typically processed within 48 to 72 hours.

08 Children's Privacy

AUREX CITY complies with Discord's minimum age requirements (13 years of age, or the local age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under this age.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. Updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage users and server administrators to review this page periodically.
